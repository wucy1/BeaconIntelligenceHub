# BIH — 2-minute community workflow (tutorial script)

**Target length:** ≤ 2 minutes  
**Audience:** UNDP evaluators / field communities

## Scene 1 — Open app (0:00–0:15)

1. Open the BIH web app (or installed PWA / Capacitor build).
2. Switch UI language if needed (EN, 繁中, 简中, AR, FR, RU, ES, DE, PT).
3. On **Home**, tap the active crisis (e.g. *Demo: Manila earthquake 2026* or *Demo: NYC coastal flood 2026*).

## Scene 2 — Photo & damage (0:15–0:45)

4. Tap **Photo** → take or choose a picture of damaged infrastructure.
5. Select damage level:
   - **Minimal / no damage**
   - **Partially damaged**
   - **Completely damaged**
6. Fill required multi-select:
   - **Type of infrastructure** (residential, commercial, government, utility, transport, community, public, other)
   - **Nature of crisis** (earthquake, flood, etc.)
7. Answer **Debris clearing required** (yes/no).

## Scene 3 — Geolocation (0:45–1:10)

8. On the map, tap a **building footprint** (highlighted polygon).
9. If GPS/building pick is unavailable, enter a **text landmark** instead.
10. Enter **infrastructure name** and a short **description** (in your language).

## Scene 4 — Submit (1:10–1:25)

11. Tap **Submit report**.
12. If offline, confirm the banner shows **saved offline**; reconnect to sync.

## Scene 5 — Dashboard & export (1:25–2:00)

13. Open **Dashboard** → enable **Latest per building** to see UNDP versioning.
14. Review analytics (damage counts, totals).
15. Download **Export CSV** or **Export GeoJSON** (latest or all).
16. (Ops) Open **Operations console** (`/ops/login`), sign in, review reports on the map or Dashboard, and run archive when needed.

## Checklist for recording

- [ ] One successful online submission visible on dashboard map/table  
- [ ] Latest view after second report on same building  
- [ ] Export file opens in GIS tool (QGIS / Excel)  
- [ ] Optional: offline queue → sync demo
